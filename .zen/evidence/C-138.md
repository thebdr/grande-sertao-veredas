# C-138 evidence - the records a read returns

## The codec (2026-11-22)

New in Veredas 2: what phase 6 reads of a device by the implicit read (C-137): I&M0 for deep
identity, and the port's real data for the device-to-device links passive LLDP cannot give (the
plan's trap). Checked against tshark 4.6.6's PNIO dissector, reading the records inside read
answers, before the tests were written.

What the dissector settled:
- **Only block version 1.0 is read** for both records: 1.1 is "not implemented yet!" at warning
  severity. The codec reads 1.0 alone and refuses another version by name.
- **The port's data aligns its fields to four bytes from the block's start** (the block type's
  first byte), not from the body: the padding after the number of peers, after a peer's chassis id,
  after its MAC, after the MAU type and after the link state.
- **A peer's delay word has a format bit**: bit 31 set, the dissector reads bits 0 to 30 as the
  cable delay (`cable_delay_value`), clear as the line delay (`line_delay_value`).
- **Bytes after the medium are ignored** by the dissector, with no complaint; the codec keeps them
  as heard, so a later version's fields are not lost on the way back.
- **The software revision's prefix is a character**: the dissector shows it quoted (`'V'`).

Before the first commit, 22 mutants of `record.rs` were run against the tests: 20 killed, two
survivors (the link's up test saw only links 1 and 2; the I&M version 1.1 read the same both ways
round), each killed by a test added.

## Refute round 1 (2026-11-22): refuted, the code right, the fixtures too quiet

The critic sent non-zero values of every field through tshark (slot 7, profile 0xF600, a tilde in
each text, a MAC of six different bytes) and read each back as written; fuzzed 400 000 bodies with
no panic and every accepted body written back to its bytes; and found every count right. But the
tests held most numbers at 0 or under a byte, so 17 mutants passed: the port's slot written as 0
or before its padding, I&M0's profile id and type swapped, the revision's internal number dropped,
a version or type checked by its low byte only, the visible range losing its tilde, a peer MAC's
middle bytes swapped, the high bytes of the MAU type, the boundaries, the medium and the vendor id
dropped. The fixtures now carry each field distinct and past a byte, tildes in every text, slot 7
in the read answers tshark reads, and versions and types differing in either byte. The 256-peer
refusal had borrowed a length error whose words were false ("255 bytes after its version"); it
has its own now. Before this commit, the critic's 17 survivors and three more were run: all
killed.

## Refute round 2 (2026-11-22): refuted, the code right, the fixtures still too quiet

The critic sent realistic values through tshark (a space-padded order id and serial, a revision
counter 0x0205, slot 0x0102, a cable delay of 0x1234 ns) and read each back as written, by the
codec and by tshark; fuzzed 500 000 bodies with no panic and every accepted body written back; and
found every count right. But 19 mutants passed: three I&M0 numbers still under a byte (the revision
counter, the profile's type, the supported records, so a device declaring I&M 12 to 15 would read
as supporting none), the version's minor byte equal to the revision's bugfix, texts trimmed on
reading (no read text had a space at either end, though I&M0's padding is spaces), delays under
256, the slot under a byte, and the text and prefix bounds one side each. The fixtures now hold
every two-byte number past a byte and the version and revision numbers all different, texts padded
with spaces read as sent, delays past 0xFFFF, slot 0x0102 (a read header at slot 7 apart from it),
and each bound tried both ways. Before this commit, eighteen of those mutants were run: all killed.

## Refute round 3 (2026-11-23): refuted, the code right, one fixture still quiet

The critic sent 40 random record pairs through tshark (every number over its full range, delays
with any bits set, random visible texts), each read field for field with no complaint and each
round-tripping; re-ran 12 of the earlier rounds' mutants and 6 of its own, all killed; found every
count right and no panic. Found, and answered:
- **I&M0's hardware revision was the same two bytes as its version** (0x0103 and 1.3), the
  functional number equal to the revision counter's high byte and the bugfix to the profile
  type's low byte: four mutants reading or writing one in another's place passed, tshark's check
  too. The fixture now holds hardware revision 0x0A0B, functional 0x11 and bugfix 0x0E, a test
  holds every number's bytes to appear once in the body, and the version 2.1 record is compared
  whole.
- **The delay word's bits 17 to 30 were never set**: masks of 20 and 27 bits, a modulo, and two
  wrong format tests passed (one reading a cable delay of 0 as a line delay). Five words at the
  edges of bits 30 and 31 are now read and written, and a three-peer record holding the largest
  line delay, a cable delay of 0 and one with bit 30 set is read by tshark.
- **Padding was refused only in two-byte pads**: a check of the first two bytes of a pad passed.
  A one-byte pad and each byte of a three-byte pad are now set.
- **Padding was named by a field that does not exist** (the defect): after the last peer "next
  peer", and with no peer "first peer", where the MAU type follows. It is named by the MAU type
  now, and the cuts of a two-peer and a no-peer body pin the names.
- **A record no block can carry was written by the record's writer** (informational in the
  report): 255 peers with 255-byte texts made a 133 906-byte body that only `Block::encode` then
  refused. `PortDataReal::block` refuses past 65 533 bytes with its own error.
- **"Exact error" and "with messages"** covered six checks that only matched the field: each is
  now the whole error, and the text errors' messages are asserted.
Before this commit, the critic's mutants and this round's, fifteen, were run: all killed.
