# C-032 evidence - controllable pressure

## The design (2026-11-06)

Pressure is the bench channel's knobs turned while the conversation runs, measured on the
other end of a real loopback socket: the rate on the unit's count of announcements received
over two-second windows (the interval's, whatever the host's tick, since every due time is
scheduled from the last due time); a fault's share on the machine's own count over a hundred
items and on what the unit still shows; every chute full on the outcomes the unit reads (FULL,
and the items coming round again), then every chute back; the no-reads on the unit's rejections
to destination 0 and the machine's count of invalid destinations; a peer gone on the
announcements stopping and the oldest unanswered item ageing.

The tolerances are the host's: a window of two seconds at 100 ms allows 17 to 23 (one tick of
jitter at each end, a status answered a tick late), the 50 % share 30 to 70 over a hundred
(three standard deviations and a half), and the counts of two ends compared to within what is in
flight. An item rejected to destination 0 is at no chute, so "every chute full" forces nothing
on it: its outcome is drawn, and the test counts outcomes at a chute only.

## Refute round 1 (2026-11-06): refuted

The engine announced at most one item per pass and skipped the due times a pass covered, so an
interval below the host's tick (20 ms on Windows' 31 ms quantum) gave two thirds of the rate,
and no window measured such an interval; `announce_now` was pinned where a regular item would
have come anyway; the invalid-destination count was bounded on one side; the 50 % share over a
hundred items could not tell 40 % from 50. Now a pass announces every item due since the last
pass, up to twenty (a longer stall skips the rest and keeps the phase), the channel carries the
fraction of a millisecond it used to truncate to the next pass, a 20 ms window is pinned at 85
to 115 in 2 s, `announce_now` at a minute's interval gives exactly one and no burst, the share
is over a thousand items at 10 ms within 44 to 56, and once every item is answered the invalid
destinations rose by exactly the no-reads. Held: the bands excluding the old drift at 100 and
50 ms, the five newest FULL, the clearing with nothing left to end FULL, the ageing of the
oldest unanswered item with items still awaiting.

## Refute round 2 (2026-11-07): holds

Over 8 s windows the unit received 80 of 80 at 100 ms, 399 of 400 at 20 ms, 801 of 800 at 10 ms
and 1145 of 1142 at 7 ms, bursts of four or five per pass answered in order with no unknown
answers; the 20 ms band excludes the old one-per-pass rate on Windows and the engine test's
31 ms tick carries Linux; `announce_now` cannot coincide with a due time; the equality of the
invalid destinations has nothing in flight at either end; the share band catches a ten-point
error and not a five-point one, which the draw's one readable line covers.
