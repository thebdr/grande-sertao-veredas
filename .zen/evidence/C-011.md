# C-011 — The GENIBELT catalog: every typed message decoded and encoded per the specification — lab notebook

## The port (2026-11-05)

From Veredas 1's `crates/veredas-telegram/src/genibelt.rs` (the catalog, 1 933 lines) and its
inline tests, now `src/telegram/genibelt.rs` and `tests/telegram_genibelt.rs`, unchanged but for
paths. The authority is the vendor specification, machine-local and not copied
(`docs/questions.md` Q6); its section numbers stay in the comments. The constant assertion
`506 <= MAX_DATA_SEGMENT` became the encoded size of the largest message against the §3.3 limits.

## Refute round 1 (2026-11-05)

The critic built golden bodies by hand from the specification's offset tables for all 32 typed
messages and the ISPC/FSG structs: every id, MSG LENGTH, offset and width matched. Refuted on:
- the output name (§4.1.1.7) went out without its trailing NUL at 26 characters or more: the
  block now carries at most 25 bytes of text, then zeros;
- bits 28-31 of a bin state (MAPPING and the three TRISTATE bits) read as no words: named now;
  whether the TRISTATE bits stop a chute the specification does not say (`docs/questions.md` Q7,
  Veredas 1's behaviour kept, `// DECISION PENDING`);
- §3.3's 512 bytes were applied to the body, though a data segment is MSG ID, MSG LENGTH and MSG
  DATA (§2.3): bodies of 509-512 bytes passed both ways. `MAX_BODY` is 508 now;
- the tests could not see a wrong layout (round trips agree with themselves) nor a wrong code
  value: the critic's golden bodies and numeric tables are added as tests;
- a UTF-16 block could end in a lone high surrogate (`"A" x 39` then an emoji in 80 bytes): a
  surrogate pair now goes out whole or not at all.

Left: `asciiz` trims trailing whitespace, so an output name `"CONV "` reads back `"CONV"` (Veredas
1 did the same; the field is NUL-padded on the wire). `MAX_PROTOCOL` (§3.3, 5 KB frames) is named
here and enforced nowhere; a frame limit belongs to the codec and the channel, a later clause.

## Refute round 2 (2026-11-05)

The round-1 fixes held (every fixed length from 0 to 700 bytes, 3 000 fuzzed bodies per id in
both orders, the NUL, the surrogate edge). Refuted on the critic's own golden values: byScnId and
byNumAlternativeDest were both 2 in the Destination body, and the ISPC block repeated two values,
so swapping those fields passed; now distinct. Also: the clause said text survives, though an
ASCIIZ field reads back without trailing spaces (now stated and pinned), and a non-ASCII output
name could end in half a UTF-8 character at the 25-byte cut (now cut on a character boundary;
non-ASCII still goes as UTF-8, as in Veredas 1). Noted: `accepts_parcels` ignores MAPPING (bit 28)
as well as the TRISTATE bits; Q7 covers bits 28-31. `szMsgToShow` carries no NUL, and the
specification does not call it ASCIIZ.

## Shelved (2026-11-05): GENIBELT left core

The operator decided that GENIBELT does not belong in core (`docs/questions.md` Q8): the vendor's
catalog, sort plan and decision path are rules a portal telegram channel is configured with, not
suite code. The module and its tests were removed from `crates/veredas-core`; the clause is
shelved, not refuted. Its last state, with every refute fix above, is commit b7c6d0a, for
whoever ports the fixes to Veredas 1.
