# C-136 evidence - the alarm frame

## The codec (2026-11-22)

New in Veredas 2: Veredas 1's aranha had nothing of the alarm exchange. Written for core's IO
controller and maquinar's PROFINET device (`docs/aranha-plan.md`, phase 3), to core's rules (no
member, no vendor, typed errors, total on input), checked against tshark 4.6.6's PNIO dissector
before the tests were written: hand-made frames, `tshark -T ek`, and `-G fields` and `-G values`
for the masks and value strings.

What the dissector settled:
- **TACK is read as the whole high nibble of AddFlags** (mask 0xF0), where the standard has TACK
  at bit 4 and bits 5 to 7 reserved: AddFlags 0xF1 reads as TACK 15, 0x31 as 3. The codec keeps the
  reserved bits as heard and names bit 4; the dissected frames keep the reserved bits clear, and
  two frames with them set pin the difference.
- **A DATA PDU with an empty var part breaks the dissector** (a failed assertion "length >= 0",
  reported as a dissector bug at error severity), a PDU type outside 1 to 4 is "Malformed", and a
  block version other than 1.0 is "not implemented" (warning). The codec keeps all three as unread
  bytes with their reason; the dissector is held to the frames written from typed parts.
- **The dissector reads past the var part**: a block length past the var part is not checked
  (block length 40 in a var part of 32 read with no complaint), and a var part of 3 bytes is read
  as a block whose length and version come from the frame's pad. The codec bounds every read by
  the var part length, so these frames are not dissected as written.
- **The PNIO status is named by its first byte**: in the `pn_io` layer its fields are
  `pn_rt_pn_io_error_code`, `..._error_decode`, then `..._error_code1` and `..._error_code2` for a
  status of zero, `..._error_code1_pnio` and `..._error_code2_pnio_<code1>` for a PNIO one (60
  "AlarmAck Error Codes", 253 "RTA_ERR_CLS_PROTOCOL", whose code 2 of 5 is "AR consumer DHT/WDT
  expired").
- The item of a notification: user structure identifier 0x8000 is read as a channel diagnosis
  (channel number, properties, error type); one under 0x8000 as the manufacturer's data.

Before the first commit, 27 mutants of `alarm.rs` (each bit mask, each nibble, each length rule,
each refusal, the round-trip gate, the helpers' flags and endpoints) were run against the tests:
all 27 killed.

## Refute round 1 (2026-11-22): refuted, one defect and two over-claims

The critic found the masks, nibbles, block layouts, the round-trip gate and every count holding,
no panic in 20 000 wider inputs, and every hand-made mutant of a rule killed. Found, and answered:
- **A decoded PDU could fail to be written back** (the defect): decode took any var part up to
  65 535 bytes when the bytes were there (a jumbo frame, or a payload handed in directly), while
  encode refuses past 1486; 2683 of 10 165 decoded wide inputs could not be written. Decode now
  refuses a var part past what a frame carries, by name, typed or not; the noise reaches 2199 bytes.
- **"The dissector reads every frame it writes" claimed too much**: only seven frames were
  dissected, and tshark complains of an item whose data does not fit its identifier's structure (a
  channel diagnosis with no data, which a test writes) and of the var parts kept unread and written
  as heard. The clause now says which frames the dissector reads whole, and the complaints are
  pinned.
- **A maintenance item hides the item it qualifies**: the codec names the first identifier and
  keeps the rest as data, where the dissector reads two identifiers. The clause and the type's
  documentation say so, and a test pins both readings.
Before this commit, three mutants of the new refusal were run: all killed.

## Refute round 2 (2026-11-22): refuted, the codec holding, two readings of the dissector over-claimed

The critic found no mutant the tests let through that breaks the clause, 200 000 noisy payloads
round-tripping, and 10 000 random typed frames read by tshark field for field with no complaint.
Answered, all in what the clause said of the dissector:
- **"No complaint" was false for statuses the dissector does not know**: 198 of 500 random statuses
  drew a warning (an ErrorDecode other than 0x00 and 0x80; under 0x81, an ErrorCode1 it has no
  table for). The clause now says statuses it knows, and two unknown ones are pinned by their
  complaints. A seeded batch of three hundred typed frames with known statuses now backs the
  general sentence, where seven hand-picked frames did.
- **The dissector complains of three unread var parts, not of all**: of nine of the test's
  twenty-six it is silent, and some it reads as typed (a notification of the other priority, as a
  notification). The clause names the three and says the dissector is no oracle for what is kept
  unread; one silent case is pinned.
- **Wording**: 70 028 is refused typed only, and the retries are no field of the PDU: both said so.
