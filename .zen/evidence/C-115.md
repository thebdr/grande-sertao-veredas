# C-115: `reflexo`'s first half

## What was built, and why so

- **Models step on the scene's thread.** A conveyor sets a belt's speed and a sensor model reads a
  scene sensor between two of the scene's steps, so a model and the bodies it moves share one
  clock and a run repeats; `fisica`'s runner takes hooks for this (C-114). Without a scene (drives
  alone) the models run every 4 ms on a thread of their own.
- **Points by name, through a view of the device.** The Modbus server and the EtherNet/IP adapter
  each give a view of their points (`ModbusPoints`, `EnipPoints`) that another thread holds: the
  same lock and image as the handle, so a model's write is what a client reads next. EtherCAT's
  process data has no named points; binding it by byte and bit waits for the second half.
- **Checked at load against the configurations**: a point's kind (bool or number) is the type its
  server would give it (a bit table or a bit is bool), so a bench file that loads has models that
  can bind.

## What the test taught

- **A ramp summed in floating point lands a step late.** 0.4 added 125 times is
  49.999999999999886, so the 126th step clamped to 50: a 0.5 s ramp took 0.504 s. Within a
  billionth of a step's change of its goal, the ramp lands on it; the test pins 50 exactly at the
  125th step, and the descent at the 62nd. (Refute round 1 found that slack too small for long
  ramps; see below.)
- **A u16 point rounds before the drive arrives**: 19.68 reads 20. The no-scene test waits for the
  drive's own speed, then for the point.
- **Sensor delays count model steps**: the model reads the scene's sensor as the last step left it,
  so a 10 ms on-delay at 4 ms steps switches at the third step that sees the body (12 ms), 20 ms
  off-delay at the fifth; pinned exactly.
- **The reference's key lists**: a bullet's keys must all sit on its first line for the bench-file
  test's reader; the conveyor's shared keys became three bullets.

## The end-to-end run

The reference's sixth example (docs/bench-schema.md) started through `Bench::start` with the
worked scene of docs/cena-schema.md beside it, driven by `tokio-modbus` as a controller would: the
conveyor reached its setpoint of 50 after its half-second ramp, the carton broke the normally
closed barrier (the input to 0) and cleared it, the pusher reached its end after its half-second
stroke; no model error in more than 500 steps.

## A wall-time bound that load broke (2026-11-23)

The end-to-end test asserted the conveyor's ramp and the pusher's stroke took at least 450 ms of
wall time. In a full run the ramp showed done after 443 ms: the scene's runner, late on the clock,
takes its missed steps at once, so the scene can run ahead of the wall clock after a stall. The
test now counts the scene's own steps between the command and what the client reads: at least 124
for the ramp (50 rounds from 49.5) and 125 for the stroke.

## Refute round 1 (2026-11-23): refuted, answered

The critic's probes and mutants are in `~/.cache/veredas/critic-c115/probes`; each finding is now a
pin in `tests/reflexo.rs`'s `round_1` (and one in `tests/fisica.rs`).

- **An actuator summed `dt / stroke` in floats** and reached an end a step late for one stroke in
  eight (40 ms in 11 steps, 300 ms in 76, some late on the way back only). Its travel is now counted
  in whole nanoseconds of the step, so an end comes at the first step at or after its time; every
  stroke from 1 ms to 1 s is pinned both ways. The sensors' delays are counted the same way (their
  millionth-of-a-step slack is gone).
- **A long ramp still landed late**: the summed error grows with the speed and the steps, past a
  billionth of a step (1200 at 30 a second landed at the 10 001st step). A ramp is now taken in
  stretches at one rate toward one goal, the speed at the k-th step of a stretch `from + k x change`
  and the goal from the step count worked out once, so no rounding gathers; ramps of up to
  107 143 steps are pinned at their step and not before.
- **A scene stepped without its models while another scene was built**: `Bench::start` started each
  scene's thread as it was built and added the models' hooks after every scene; with a second scene
  of 20 000 bodies the line's scene took 281 steps against its models' 25, its belt running on the
  scene file's 0.5 m/s. `fisica::paused` now builds a scene that runs once `run` is called; the
  bench builds every scene, adds the hooks, then runs them.
- **Unpinned claims**, now pinned: the models' step is the scene's (a 10 ms scene), their order by
  kind and by file, at speed while reversed, a delay counted without a break, the 4 ms without a
  scene (a step never early, at most 200 ms behind), the refusals of an empty name, a NaN or
  infinite ramp, a stroke past 600000 and a NaN ratio by their text, the missing scene file's text
  with its path.
- **Wording**: a step with several failing models is counted once (the clause said "counted with
  the last error", read as per error); an f32 point now writes NaN as 0, as the clause said of every
  number.
- Also: `ReflexoHandle::stop` now waits for a step under way (the stop flag is read under the
  models' lock), so no model step runs after it returns, with a scene or without.

## Refute round 2 (2026-11-23): refuted, answered

Round 1's answers held. The critic's probes and mutants are in `~/.cache/veredas/critic-c115-r2/probes`;
each finding is a pin in the test's `round_2` module or in the refusal test.
- **A point two parts' models wrote loaded**: `validate` kept its written-point list per part, so
  two `[[reflexo]]` parts could both write `m.speed`, their writes alternating on it. The bench now
  keeps one list for the whole file (`validate_beside`), the refusal naming the other part's place.
- **An actuator went on writing its other switch after one write failed**, where a drive stops at
  its first: it is now skipped for the rest of its step, as the clause says of any model.
- **A ramp of about 21 million steps or more (about a day) landed a step late**: the count's slack
  of a billionth of a step is passed by the division's rounding once the count passes 2^24. The
  slack is now relative to the count (`reflexo::ramp_steps`); over 1 376 235 ramps (setpoints 1 to
  65535 at 21 rates) none lands late or early against the exact time, worked out in rationals.
- **Unpinned claims, each with a surviving mutant**: the order of conveyors, actuators and sensors
  and within each kind; the adapter's u16, i16, u32, i32 and f32 conversions (round 1's NaN as 0
  among them); a tolerance not finite, a ratio infinite, an off delay, the retracted pose, a switch
  written twice or of the other kind; a stop waiting for a scene's step under way.
- **Wording**: the refusal count is the test's (it said 31 for 30; now 38).

## Refute round 3 (2026-11-23): refuted, answered

Round 2's answers held. The critic's probes and mutants are in `~/.cache/veredas/critic-c115-r3/probes`;
each finding is a pin in the test's `round_3` module.
- **An infinite setpoint was reached in one step**: an f32 point a client sets to +inf made the
  ramp's count `inf - inf`, NaN, held to 1 step; the speed went to inf, and a setpoint put back was
  reached in one step more. The count is now held at the largest (`u64::MAX`) and the ramp goes on
  at its rate: 0.4 a step at 100/s, back to 50 at the deceleration.
- **A ramp through 0 landed a step late**: each half was rounded up to a step of its own, the drive
  resting a step on 0 (its running point 0 while its run was on); 252 of 441 pairs of rates landed a
  step after their time. A reversal is now one ramp of two rates: the step that passes 0 carries
  what is left of it at the acceleration, and the whole lands at the first step at or after the sum
  of its two times (the main test's reversal now 0.2 at its 63rd step and on 50 at its 188th).
- **Wording**: two `[[reflexo]]` parts moving one scene step one after the other in the file's
  order (a conveyor run by the next part's drive starts a step behind it), now said and pinned both
  ways; at speed is the run on and the speed within its tolerance, a setpoint of 0 included while
  not running, now said and pinned.
- **Unpinned claims, each with a surviving mutant**: rounding (not truncating) on seven integer
  types; the belt's hold at +100 m/s; a conveyor whose point write fails leaving its belt; an
  actuator whose read fails skipped; the off delay counted without a break.
- Held: a stroke of -1 or a delay past u32 is refused by line and column as every bench key is.

## Refute round 4 (2026-11-25): refuted, answered

changes. The critic's probes and mutants are in `~/.cache/veredas/critic-c115-r4/probes`; each finding

- **A reversal's exact zero step read a speck of speed**: where the deceleration ends exactly on a

- **Unpinned claims, each with a surviving mutant**: a setpoint changed mid-reversal (a ramp through
  0 kept whatever changed), a reversed drive's deceleration (a negative speed decelerating at the
  acceleration), a drive skipped for the rest of its step after a failed read or write, models on

  pitch and roll, nine point refusals.

  name neither empty nor padded (the reader refused all three); the slack is relative to the count
  and a billionth of a step besides.
