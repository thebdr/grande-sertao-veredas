# C-020: One poll into rows and samples: lab notebook

## The port (2026-11-06)

From Veredas 1's `crates/veredas-opc/src/map.rs` and its inline tests, now `src/opc/map.rs` and
`tests/opc_map.rs`.

What changed:
- **A bad read of a word polled only for its bits is reported.** It goes on the first bit
  column. Veredas 1 reported a bad read only on the tag's own column, so a bits-only word went
  silent: holes, and no failure.
- **A good status that carried no value is reported as `no value`,** not as `Good`.
- **`render_line` is one line.** A newline in a text tag broke the line Veredas 1 called
  one-line.
- **`RuleRow` derives `PartialEq`,** so the tests compare whole rows.
- **The docs name no caller.** Veredas 1's named the event feed, the trailing screen, a decision
  and a plant.

New tests:
- the samples field by field;
- holes across bit columns;
- the absent node;
- bits of a float;
- the heartbeat at its exact polls;
- one rule skipped beside another that keeps;
- a hole as a transition;
- a spec that gains a rule.

## Refute round 1 (2026-11-06): refuted

- **An empty variant beside a good status was reported once per column.**
  - It is reachable: the stack decodes a null value as `Some(Variant::Empty)`.
  - It is now treated as no value: in the mapping, in `tag_reads` (C-022) and in
    `TagRead::is_good`.
  - Pinned on a tag with bits and on a bits-only word.
- **A NaN never counted as unchanged** (`Value` derives `PartialEq`). A float stuck at NaN, a
  faulted sensor, turned storing on change off for its rule.
  - Rows are now compared with NaN equal to NaN.
  - Pinned: kept once, then at the heartbeat, with a number and NaN again each a transition.
- **A bad tag mapped by two rules is reported in each.** The clause now says so ("once per tag
  in each rule that maps it"), and it is pinned.
- **U+2028 and U+2029 now read as spaces too.** The refuter noted they passed through; a
  consumer may break lines on them.

Held:
- lookup by node, positions, samples field by field, holes without samples;
- the heartbeat at exactly polls 60 and 120;
- a skipped rule still reporting.

## Refute round 2 (2026-11-06): refuted, one test gap

All four round-1 findings are closed. The new float comparison in `same_row` was exercised only
with NaN. Deleting its `x == y` would have kept every poll of a steady float, silently turning
storing on change off for every analog value.

Now pinned:
- a steady 21.5 kept once, then at the heartbeat;
- a rule that gained a trailing column kept (the length check).

Held: the line separators, the empty variant on every path, and NaN in both directions.
