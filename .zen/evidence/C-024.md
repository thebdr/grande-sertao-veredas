# C-024 evidence - veredas-maquinar, the bench

## The port, phase 1 (2026-11-06): the crate and the emitters

Read before deciding: all of Veredas 1's `crates/veredas-maquinar` (`config.rs`, `logsim.rs`,
`opcsim.rs`, `tcpsim.rs`, `mscsim.rs`, `pnsim.rs`, `app.rs`, `ui_logs.rs`, `ui_opc.rs`,
`ui_pn.rs`, `ui_tcp.rs`, the six test files, `examples/fake_eu.rs`, the empty `pki/` folders)
and the automated replies of `crates/veredas-trajetorias` (`plan.rs`, `service.rs`; `store.rs`
and its DuckDB are not carried: the plan's decision 2), plus the sort plan engine
(`veredas-telegram/src/sortplan.rs`) the replies rested on, and Veredas 1's plan section
"Tooling — the plant simulator". What moves where is the table of `docs/maquinar-plan.md`.

The crate at `2.0.0-alpha.1`, listed in the root manifest; depends on core (with `demo-server`,
for the relay the OPC phase uses) and on the OPC UA server stack, chrono, serde, toml, thiserror
and tokio, every one already in `[workspace.dependencies]`; no new external crate.

`tests/component.rs` is core's `tests/foundation.rs` with one difference: the bench may build one
binary, named `veredas-maquinar` (the executable of phase 5), so the rule is one library, at most
one binary of that name, and tests. The planted cases add a `src/main.rs` and a `[[bin]]` of the
package's name (accepted), a binary of another name and two binaries (refused), and a package
with a binary and no library (refused). The vendor reader is core's, word for word, with one more
planted sample: a vendor term in a TOML fixture's comment, since the bench's tests read fixtures.
