## Refute round 1 (2026-11-05)

Refuted, two bugs inherited from Veredas 1:
- `int_in` subtracted in `i64`: any range wider than `i64::MAX` panicked (debug) or wrapped out of
  range (release). Veredas 1's bench feeds it user-typed f64 bounds, which `as i64` saturates, so
  a bound of 1e19 killed the simulator thread. Now in `i128`.
- `uniform` computed `lo + u * (hi - lo)`: two finite bounds whose width overflows gave an
  infinite span, every draw at `hi`, and NaN when `u` is 0. Now `lo * (1 - u) + hi * u`, and the
  decimal rounding is skipped where scaling would overflow.
Also refuted: replay was asserted on the first value only and nothing pinned the output; the
reference demo's sequence for seed 42 stream 54 is pinned now, and a thousand mixed draws replay.
The clause said streams diverge; as in the reference, a stream id is taken modulo 2^63 (stream 0
and 2^63 are the same), now said and pinned. `next_f64` is public (the sort plan draws from it).

Held: the constants, warm-up and XSH-RR output against the reference; streams 0..4095 distinct;
an inverted range swapped; 400 000 fuzzed `uniform` draws in range; `flip` balanced.
