# C-156 evidence - the front end

New in Veredas 2: Veredas 1 had no interpreter. Built on 2026-11-26 and 27 from `docs/orcl/README.md` ("The front
end"), the study `docs/orcl/front-end.md` (whose spike driver was not kept) and `rustc_public` as the pinned nightly
has it (its source read at the nightly's own commit, c7d5ba59d). The lowering was written by a subagent of the
controlador member in a worktree of its own, from a brief naming the clause, and landed by the member after the
gates below were run again on the member's tree.

## The first increment (2026-11-27)
On stable, `cargo build -p orcl-front`, clippy and `cargo nextest run -p orcl-front -p leis` (73) green: the shell
holds `NIGHTLY` alone, `orcl-interp` and `orcl-std` being optional dependencies of the `driver` feature. On
`nightly-2026-11-21`, clippy with the feature clean and the lowering test green (20 tests, about 1 s).

Choices the brief left to the builder, kept:
- **Flags**: `-Zalways-encode-mir -Zmir-opt-level=0` on every crate, no `-Cpanic=abort`: with no drop glue no block
  unwinds (every edge is "unwind continue"), and a crate built to abort would not link into a build that unwinds.
- **`usize` and `isize`**: refused in what a program declares (fields, parameters, results, named variables, array
  elements), allowed in the compiler's own temporaries (an index, a discriminant read).
- **The refused type named** is the innermost one (`[u8]`, not `&[u8]`).
- **The pre-write check** loads the program with `Limits { stack: 0, memory: 1 << 24 }`.
- **Beyond `rustc_public`**, the driver reads the compiler's `TyCtxt` (through `run_with_tcx!`) for lang and
  diagnostic items, item and crate names, an inherent impl's own type and macro call sites, which `rustc_public`
  does not give; a move of the nightly (Q222) is gated by this suite.
- **What the interpreter has no instruction for** is built from what it has: an array of one value repeated becomes
  an aggregate of its copies (bounded at 65 536), `Len` of an array a constant, and a promoted `&CONST` a local
  holding the constant and a reference to it, the interpreter keeping no static memory.

Gaps of the closed list met (Q227: the list grows with its conformance programs, never by interpreting core), each
refused by name today: `<[T; N] as Default>::default` (a derived `Default` over an array field; the `shapes`
program writes its own), `<&T as PartialEq>::eq` (a derived `PartialEq` on an enum with data), `Range` (a `for` over
a range), `From` and `TryFrom`, `Ord::clamp`, a slice's `get`, `Option::unwrap_or`. One gap blocked every program
holding a timer, `Default` of an `Option`, and was closed in the interpreter (C-153, 2026-11-27) before landing.

The clause is pending its first refute round, which waits for C-153's, C-155's and C-152's (one critic at a time).

## Refute round 1 (2026-11-27): refuted, three high gaps and three defects
At 29475e3b, the recorded command green on the pristine copy (17 s), every mutant under a memory scope of its own. Of
44 valid mutants of `src/`, 12 killed and 32 passed. Coverage of the driver over the lowering test showed what no
conformance program reaches: `>` never, `<=` once, a `Drop` terminator, a one-variant enum's layout, a niche of more
than one niche variant, a constant reference at an offset, the `isize` refusal, a crate compiled with the program's
options set under another name. The lowering itself was right on everything the critic probed (comparisons at their
edges, negative values and discriminants, two-variant niches, generic drops, `i16` operations, ordinary control
code); what passed is in the tests:
- **high**: `>` lowered as `>=` or `<`, `<=` as `<` (m01 to m03): a program's `if level > max` would fire at equality
  or inverted against its native build, and no test would see it;
- **moderate**: `!=` mapped wrong (m06); a niche constant of a second niche variant decoded as the first (m24); a
  crate with two `Program`s lowered silently (m29); a drop test inverted refusing sound generic code (m09); a switch on
  a negative value, or a negative discriminant, refused at load (m07, m22); the environment's program name matched to
  every crate compiled (m30b);
- **low**: other constant shapes (m25, m26b, m27); the source map inside core's macros and a derive's expansion (m35,
  m40b); every fault but division by zero misnamed (m10 to m13); statements and calls at their function's line (m14,
  m15); each declared `usize`/`isize` but a field lowered (m18 to m21); the loader's verdict ignored and a file written
  for a crate rustc refuses after its analysis (m33, m39); an inherent `i16` operation taken as `i32` (m36);
- **contrived**: four (an index from the end, an option winning over the environment, exactly 65 536 copies, an
  unreachable block lowered as a return).

Three real defects of the `usize` rule at 29475e3b, low: a `(usize, u8)` field and a `&usize` parameter lowered, not
refused, and an `Option<usize>` field refused at core's `option.rs`, not at the program's field. The critic's kill
tests and programs are kept in `~/.cache/veredas/controlador/c156-r1/probes/`; their adoption and the fix come next.

## Round 1 answered (2026-11-27)
The fix, by a subagent in a worktree of its own and landed by the member after the gates were run again: the
`usize`/`isize` rule is now deep (`src/types.rs`): a declared type is walked through its structures' and tuples'
fields, its enums' payloads, its arrays' elements and its references' pointees (an enum's discriminant is the
compiler's, not walked), and a word found is refused at the program's declaration, named as the innermost type; an
`Option`'s own fields are core's and no longer checked, its payload refused where the program declares the `Option`.
One more change a kill test showed: the first-sight check of an array's element refused a `[usize; 2]` at a
caller's temporary rather than at the parameter, and is gone, an array's element now refused at the declaration like
every other word and a `[usize; N]` living only in an unnamed temporary allowed like any temporary. The choice this
note first recorded ("refused in fields, parameters, results, named variables and array elements") now reads:
wherever they stand in a declared type, at the declaration. The critic's 17 kill tests and three gap tests are
adopted into `tests/lowering.rs` (43 tests, from 20), with three more for the rule as a whole, and their programs
under `tests/programs/` and `tests/programs/refused/`. Replayed against the answer, the round's mutants were killed
but m20, whose code the answer removed.
