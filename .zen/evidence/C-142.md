# C-142 evidence - the listener

## The listener (2026-11-24)

Phase 4's fourth piece: the wire seam (C-139), the observations (C-140) and fusion (C-141) put to
work. Absorbed from Veredas 1's `engine.rs` (frames kept, decoded, fused, counted, the own-machine
filter) and `capture.rs` (a thread per adapter, the one paced sender), changed on the way:
- **One worker per wire, the only sender on it**: a frame handed to the listener is queued for its
  interface and sent by that worker, paced and bounded, so asking happens where it is heard and
  nowhere else (the plan's "it listens before it asks"); Veredas 1 had one sender for all.
- **What it sends is kept**, marked sent, beside what it hears: a capture of the listening holds
  the questions with the answers.
- **This machine is its wires' MACs**: an observation about one of them is left out and counted
  (Veredas 1 took the adapters' MACs from the system); the seam already never hands back a frame
  the host sent (C-139), so this covers two of this machine's interfaces on one segment.
- **Damage kept with its frame's number and interface**, the last fifty, as Veredas 1's errors; a
  wire that refuses a send is counted per interface with its reason among the damage.
- **A wire's error ends that worker only**, said in its interface's account; a capture read to its
  end says so.
- Kept frames numbered from 0 for the listener's life, the oldest let go past the bound with their
  numbers no longer kept (Veredas 1's `frame_base`).

Before the first commit, 16 mutants of `listen.rs` were run: 15 killed, and a refused send's count
killed once a wire refusing every send was added to the tests.
