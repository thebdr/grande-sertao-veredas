# C-142 evidence - the listener

## The listener (2026-11-24)

Phase 4's fourth piece: the wire seam (C-139), the observations (C-140) and fusion (C-141) put to
work. Absorbed from Veredas 1's `engine.rs` (frames kept, decoded, fused, counted, the own-machine
filter) and `capture.rs` (a thread per adapter, the one paced sender), changed on the way:
- **One worker per wire, the only sender on it**: a frame handed to the listener is queued for its
  interface and sent by that worker, paced and bounded, so asking happens where it is heard and
  nowhere else (the plan's "it listens before it asks"); Veredas 1 had one sender for all.
- **What it sends is kept**, marked sent, beside what it hears: a capture of the listening holds
  the questions with the answers.
- **This machine is its wires' MACs**: an observation about one of them is left out and counted
  (Veredas 1 took the adapters' MACs from the system); the seam already never hands back a frame
  the host sent (C-139), so this covers two of this machine's interfaces on one segment.
- **Damage kept with its frame's number and interface**, the last fifty, as Veredas 1's errors; a
  wire that refuses a send is counted per interface with its reason among the damage.
- **A wire's error ends that worker only**, said in its interface's account; a capture read to its
  end says so.
- Kept frames numbered from 0 for the listener's life, the oldest let go past the bound with their
  numbers no longer kept (Veredas 1's `frame_base`).

Before the first commit, 16 mutants of `listen.rs` were run: 15 killed, and a refused send's count
killed once a wire refusing every send was added to the tests.

## The first refute round, answered (2026-11-24)

The critic (its own copy, captures of up to 3 000 frames under five bounds replayed twice, hub
sweeps of the own filter, pacing measured where the wire is handed each frame, eight senders at
once, 38 mutants) refuted the clause; every gap is answered in the code or the tests:
- **A send on an interface whose worker had ended was taken and left queued for ever**, and what
  was queued when a worker ended was never said. A send there is refused now with the reason;
  when a worker ends or the listener stops, what is still queued is counted never sent, the queue
  emptied, and whoever handed it over told so (the listener's `Outcome`, C-144's use of it).
- **An interface's account was keyed by the name its frames carried**, so a capture recorded as
  one name and replayed as another counted nothing on its wire: every frame heard is kept and
  counted under the name of the wire that heard it.
- **This machine's own LLDP agent** (its frame from one of its MACs, naming another of its MACs
  for its chassis) was fused as a device: an observation is this machine's when its frame comes
  from one of the wires' MACs, as well as when it is about one.
- **Untested promises**: the defaults; the pace measured as an upper bound and the first frame
  at once (at two and at twenty a second, where the wire is handed each frame); the snapshot's
  conflicts; the account after a send and the sent frame's stamp; the worker reading no more after
  its wire's error; frames of 14 and 65 536 bytes; a frame of two damages one damaged frame; the
  earliest and latest stamps of a capture out of order (the clause now says earliest and latest).

Of 27 mutants of the answer (the critic's survivors and the new paths), 26 are killed; one is
equivalent (an account keyed by the kept frame's name, which is now always the wire's).

## The second refute round, answered (2026-11-25)

The critic (480 listener runs over captures of up to 4 950 frames under six bounds replayed twice,
own-filter and hub sweeps, races of four senders against failing wires, stops and captures ending,
400 000 mutated frames through decode and fusion, 59 mutants) found the hearing side and the send
accounting holding everywhere, every tracked send told exactly once, and refuted on what the
pacing and the tests left open:
- **"The first at once, no slower" held only while the poll was under half a gap**: a frame
  queued while the worker waited on its wire went when the wait ended, up to a whole poll (20 ms)
  late, one to four gaps at 40 to 200 frames a second. No wait is longer than half a gap now (the
  poll bounded by it), and the rate is bounded to a thousand frames a second; the clause says
  "within the poll, at most half a gap".
- **The pace was proven against a burst queued at start alone**: a pacer catching up after a
  quiet spell, one 17 % slow and one holding the first frame a third of a gap all passed. The test
  measures after a quiet second, over twenty gaps, at a hundred a second, and across refused
  frames.
- **A worker that panicked ended silently**, its queue untold and its interface taking sends: its
  panic is caught, said as why it ended (with the message), and its queue said never sent.
- **Sent and refused outcomes, and "told once", were unasserted**: each now, the channel closed
  after.
- **A wire named with a NUL made `start` panic** and left the other workers running: a thread's
  name drops the NUL.
- Two stale doc lines (the tracked send's "nothing comes if the worker ends", the refused count's
  "last reason") are put right.

Of 19 mutants of the answer (the critic's survivors and the new paths, each built outside the
suite lock and all run under one hold of it), all are killed; the unbounded poll once eleven
single frames were handed over at a thousand a second and their median delay measured (one
first frame at a hundred a second could not tell 5 ms from 20).

## For C-144's first refute round (2026-11-25)

Asking's critic found two holes in what the listener offers an asker, answered here: a tracked
frame's ticket now says how many frames were queued ahead of it, counted under the queue's own
lock, and a frame still waiting may be withdrawn (never sent, counted, told nothing); a frame its
worker is handing to its wire is held where the worker's end tells it, and counts it never sent,
so a panic in the wire's send leaves no tracked frame untold. The kept frames after a number are
read with how many were let go at one moment (`read_from`). Each is tested in the listener's own
test and killed by its mutants (eight of them among C-144's twenty).

## The third refute round, answered (2026-11-25)

The critic (its own copy, an accounting sweep of 150 seeds of failing, refusing, stalling and
panicking wires with concurrent senders and withdrawers, ticket counts under concurrent senders,
738 496 reads during a flood, the pace and the poll measured where the wire is handed each frame;
21 mutants) found the code holding under every sweep (every account adding up, every tracked frame
told exactly once or, withdrawn, told nothing) and refuted the test, which 15 mutants passed:
- **`read_from` was untested in this clause's test** (only asking's used it): it is read after a
  bound let frames go, and during a flood past the bound.
- **The pace's upper bound had 100 ms of slack over twenty gaps and none at a thousand a second**:
  the span is held within 30 ms at two to a thousand a second, and the timed tests run with the
  machine to themselves in the suite (`.config/nextest.toml`), as maquinar's do.
- **"Within the poll" was measured at a thousand a second alone**: single frames handed over mid-
  wait are measured at fifty and at two a second too, where a poll of a whole gap or of half a
  gap ignoring the 20 ms default shows.
- **The ticket's count ahead was checked from one thread**: eight senders queue at once.
- **A withdrawn frame's account and the wire's side were not checked**: a device on the hub never
  sees its bytes, and the whole account is asserted.
- **"Told once" was checked with `try_recv` straight after the outcome**, before the worker drops
  its sender: under load it failed red (never a false green). It waits for the channel to close.
- The poll's bound is asked of the worker directly too: a wire that records every wait it is asked
  to make shows each at most the lesser of the poll and half a gap, and an idle worker asking
  exactly that, at two to a thousand a second (the medians alone could not tell a poll of 10 ms
  from one of 20 at fifty a second).

All 15 of the critic's survivors run against the strengthened test (built outside the suite lock,
run under one hold): all killed.

## For C-144's second refute round (2026-11-26)

A tracked frame's outcome was told after the worker let go of its lock (after a send, and as a
worker ended), so whoever found the frame gone from its queue (a failed withdrawal) could find
nothing told yet. Every outcome is now told before the lock goes; asking's race test, six requests
whose worker panics as their turn runs out under a reader holding the lock, holds it.
