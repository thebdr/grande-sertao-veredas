# C-142 evidence - the listener

## The listener (2026-11-24)

Phase 4's fourth piece: the wire seam (C-139), the observations (C-140) and fusion (C-141) put to
work. Absorbed from Veredas 1's `engine.rs` (frames kept, decoded, fused, counted, the own-machine
filter) and `capture.rs` (a thread per adapter, the one paced sender), changed on the way:
- **One worker per wire, the only sender on it**: a frame handed to the listener is queued for its
  interface and sent by that worker, paced and bounded, so asking happens where it is heard and
  nowhere else (the plan's "it listens before it asks"); Veredas 1 had one sender for all.
- **What it sends is kept**, marked sent, beside what it hears: a capture of the listening holds
  the questions with the answers.
- **This machine is its wires' MACs**: an observation about one of them is left out and counted
  (Veredas 1 took the adapters' MACs from the system); the seam already never hands back a frame
  the host sent (C-139), so this covers two of this machine's interfaces on one segment.
- **Damage kept with its frame's number and interface**, the last fifty, as Veredas 1's errors; a
  wire that refuses a send is counted per interface with its reason among the damage.
- **A wire's error ends that worker only**, said in its interface's account; a capture read to its
  end says so.
- Kept frames numbered from 0 for the listener's life, the oldest let go past the bound with their
  numbers no longer kept (Veredas 1's `frame_base`).

Before the first commit, 16 mutants of `listen.rs` were run: 15 killed, and a refused send's count
killed once a wire refusing every send was added to the tests.

## The first refute round, answered (2026-11-24)

The critic (its own copy, captures of up to 3 000 frames under five bounds replayed twice, hub
sweeps of the own filter, pacing measured where the wire is handed each frame, eight senders at
once, 38 mutants) refuted the clause; every gap is answered in the code or the tests:
- **A send on an interface whose worker had ended was taken and left queued for ever**, and what
  was queued when a worker ended was never said. A send there is refused now with the reason;
  when a worker ends or the listener stops, what is still queued is counted never sent, the queue
  emptied, and whoever handed it over told so (the listener's `Outcome`, C-144's use of it).
- **An interface's account was keyed by the name its frames carried**, so a capture recorded as
  one name and replayed as another counted nothing on its wire: every frame heard is kept and
  counted under the name of the wire that heard it.
- **This machine's own LLDP agent** (its frame from one of its MACs, naming another of its MACs
  for its chassis) was fused as a device: an observation is this machine's when its frame comes
  from one of the wires' MACs, as well as when it is about one.
- **Untested promises**: the defaults; the pace measured as an upper bound and the first frame
  at once (at two and at twenty a second, where the wire is handed each frame); the snapshot's
  conflicts; the account after a send and the sent frame's stamp; the worker reading no more after
  its wire's error; frames of 14 and 65 536 bytes; a frame of two damages one damaged frame; the
  earliest and latest stamps of a capture out of order (the clause now says earliest and latest).

Of 27 mutants of the answer (the critic's survivors and the new paths), 26 are killed; one is
equivalent (an account keyed by the kept frame's name, which is now always the wire's).
