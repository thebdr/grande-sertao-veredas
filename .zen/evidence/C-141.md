# C-141 evidence - fusion

## The devices (2026-11-24)

Phase 4's third piece: observations (C-140) into devices. Absorbed from Veredas 1's `fusion.rs`
(`/home/thebdr/code/Veredas`, branch `supervisao-plan`), changed on the way:
- **A name is never a key** (the plan's rule): Veredas 1 matched an LLDP local chassis id to a
  station name it already knew; here a device is the MAC an observation names, and nothing else.
  A test holds a local chassis id equal to a known station name joining nothing.
- **A shutdown PDU joins its port's device by MAC**: found while writing the tests. A shutdown PDU
  holds the mandatory three alone (802.1AB, C-133), so a PROFINET device's leaving frame carries no
  chassis MAC, and keyed by its source it made a device of the port's MAC while the real port
  never left. Its source is a MAC already on record as that device's port: fused there, when
  exactly one device holds it.
- **Current by time, not by order applied**: the value heard last by the frame's stamp, then its
  index, so a capture merged out of order reads the same (made whole by the first refute round); every value keeps its first and last
  witness and its count (Veredas 1 kept the same, with millisecond stamps).
- **Ports keep fields with provenance** too (Veredas 1 kept the latest value), and when the port
  said it was leaving, cleared by a later frame only.
- **The device's own word on its address is a field** (Veredas 1 kept the latest), and the
  conflict it flags is read from the current one.
- **Duplicate addresses are read from current values** (Veredas 1 read every address ever held, so
  a device that moved kept its old address in conflict with its successor's).
- Conflicts are typed and ordered (kind, value), their values a name, an address, or the names
  and addresses heard in order.
- The guard that skipped a request's identity was redundant (an asker's identity is empty) and is
  gone; a mutant of it was equivalent.

Before the first commit, 22 mutants of `fusion.rs` were run: 18 killed, two killed once tests were
added (two hearings of one stamp with the earlier value in the later frame; a label with both a
station and a system name), one equivalent (the last witness updated on an equal place, which only
the same frame twice reaches) and one removed with the redundant guard.

## The first refute round, answered (2026-11-24)

The critic (a copy of its own, 6 000 random observation sets each applied in nine orders against
an oracle written from the clause, 30 mutants) refuted the clause; every gap is answered in the
code or in the clause's words, each by a test:
- **A port's leaving followed the order applied**: an older leaving applied after a later frame
  set it, and one stamp's tie ignored the index. A port now keeps the place (stamp, index) of its
  last leaving frame and of its last other one, and it has left when the first is the later.
- **The candidates' order, and with it name-changed's and ip-changed's values, followed the order
  applied**: candidates are kept in the order first heard by time (their first witness), so two
  fusions of the same observations are equal in whatever order they were applied (the test applies
  nine observations reversed and in twenty shuffles).
- **ip-changed was raised by a mask or a gateway, and by 0.0.0.0 then an address**: it compares
  the addresses alone, 0.0.0.0 none; name-changed likewise counts non-empty names only, as the
  label and duplicate-name already did.
- **An empty station name gave an empty label**: an empty name is none, the system name or the
  MAC used instead.
- **Management addresses were a set without witnesses**, every address ever given kept, so a
  moved address stayed a duplicate: they are a field like any, each PDU's set a value, the
  current one the PDU heard last.
- **A PDU naming its source as its chassis was redirected** into the device whose port the source
  was: only a PDU naming no MAC for its chassis (neither PROFINET's chassis MAC nor a chassis id
  of subtype MAC) is.
- **A leaving heard before its device made a device of the port's MAC** that then took every
  later leaving: such a device is kept as a port not yet known (`by_source`), counted no device's
  owner, and taken in by its device once a PDU from that MAC names it; a port's owner is the device
  any of whose port's frames came from the MAC, not only its current one.
- **Fifteen mutants the linked test let through** (interfaces, first and last times applied out of
  order, delays, MRP, MAU type, current words for conflicts and addresses) are killed by the new
  tests; the two equivalent ones (the same frame applied twice) stay so. Of 43 mutants of the answer, 41
  are killed; two are equivalent (a port not yet known holds LLDP alone, and its device already
  heard LLDP in the PDU that names it; an equal first witness needs the same frame twice).

Left named in the clause: a MAC that speaks for itself (DCP, ARP) before it is known as another
device's port keeps what it said by LLDP, and a MAC two devices' ports share goes by what was
applied when each PDU came.

## The second refute round, answered (2026-11-25)

The critic (its own copy, 40 000 generated sets each applied in twelve orders against an oracle
deciding ownership from the whole set, 103 mutants) found every set outside the clause's
exceptions read alike in every order, and refuted on three points:
- **An order dependence the clause did not except**: a MAC named as one device's chassis and also
  another device's port (a collision of MACs). The PDUs of it naming no chassis MAC that came
  before it was known as the port split between the two, and once its own device held such a PDU
  the MAC had two owners, so every later leaving stayed on it and the port's device never left,
  even in time order. A MAC's own device is no owner of that MAC now, so a later leaving goes to
  the port's device; what came before the port was known stays the MAC's own, and the clause's
  exception says so ("a device of its own: it speaks DCP or ARP, or a PDU names it as its
  chassis").
- **The take-in of a port not yet known was unasserted**: seventeen mutants (its leaving, its
  times, its fields, a port of another short name, the take-in of a device that speaks for
  itself) passed the linked test. The critic's seven cases are in the test, each applied in both
  orders against the time order.
- **A conflict flagged on 0.0.0.0 named "0.0.0.0"**: no address is named now, as `addresses()`
  holds none.

Of 63 mutants run against the answer (the first round's, the critic's survivors and the new
paths), 60 are killed and two are equivalent (an equal first witness, which needs the same frame
twice; the heard set of a port not yet known, LLDP alone, which its device already holds). The
last showed the owner's own check of ports not yet known redundant once a MAC's own device is no
owner (such a port is kept at its own MAC, its frames' only source), and that check is gone.
