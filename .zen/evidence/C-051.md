## Refute round 2 (2026-11-10, at 77c6737): refuted, two defects and four gaps

defects in the page as it stood. An element moved by a transform of its own or of a group around it

`use` of the group, which a clip path may not draw, so its fill could never show.

The gaps: the accent check ran once, at first paint, by exact string, so a state reached later

was never read (tags at the origin, or 400 px below a marker, passed) nor whether anything was
visible (fills, markers and tags hidden by `visibility` passed); the sparkline was only counted (one

alarm (alarm weighing least passed).

The answers. The page places tags, labels and value texts through the transforms between the

feed gives each key of a keyed marker its own text, and the side panel shows the selected key's.
The test grows the reference: outlet 1 a group with a disabled state, outlet 2 moved by its own
transform, outlet 3 inside a moved group, a keyed marker over a log's positions forgetting a key
after 6 s. It reads screen boxes: each tag centred under its element, each label over it, each fill
inside its element's box to its level, each marker and clone at its point; it checks every one
shown by computed style and hit-tests each fill, marker and clone at its centre, so a hidden or
clipped-away one fails. The accent check runs after every settle against the accent, its tint, soft
and outline by red, green and blue. The infeed is driven into fault and outlet 1 emptied: the
attention list reads alarm, warn, near, disabled, stale. The sparkline's request is read through the

per key with its tag; a click on A1 selects it alone, "Tracked · A1" with its own 10, then 30 once

Every mutation of the critic's, and five more, rebuilt and run (`scratchpad/tools/c051r2-mutants.cjs`):
