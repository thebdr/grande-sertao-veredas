# C-052 evidence - the dashboards

## The design (2026-11-09)

Veredas 1's dashboard posted a layout to the gateway and computed nothing: the page charted raw
windows with uPlot. Here a panel is TOML in the binding and every number it shows is computed in
Rust from the session's store (`panel.rs`), so the test asserts each bucket, breach, run, median
and bin exactly from lines it writes with chosen stamps; the page draws plain SVG. A window's right
edge is a parameter (`to`, the store's now by default), which is what makes the numbers exact in a
test. Bars are aligned on the clock and the bucket holding `to` is the running one. State over time
reads each sample of the object's own series by the rules over its own value (a rule over another
series has no history to read here). Charts in cells read a table's rows over the window (at most
the newest 50 000, said when capped), group by a key column, and give per key the median, σ
(population), n, the median per bucket and twenty bins from median − spread to median + spread.

## Verified in the browser (2026-11-09)

Against the demo plant of C-051: the eleven panels drawn and refreshed every five seconds, the four
tiles (normal against a target with its sub-line, "slow", the infeed's code), the trend with its
dashed limit and breach dots and a hover value, the bars per minute with the running one outlined
and the dashed target, the infeed's states over time, the grid, the levels with their ticks, the
table sorted by count with inline bars and "■ slow" words, and the charts in cells per outlet.
