# C-007 evidence - veredas-core, the foundation

## The port, phase 1 (2026-11-05): foundation and shared types

Read before deciding: all of Veredas 1's `crates/veredas-telegram` (codec, genibelt, channel,
capture, sortplan, decision_client, rng) and `crates/veredas-opc` (spec, value, map, browse,
worker, demo, the demo_server example, three end-to-end test files), and the slice of Veredas 1's
`veredas-core` the OPC layer imports: `SourceId`, `Value`, `SeriesType`, `Sample`, `now_unix_ms`,
`SourceHandle`, `stop_flag`, `SourceError` (the whole import list, by `grep veredas_core` over the
two crates). Who uses what in Veredas 1, by `grep veredas_telegram` over its crates: the codec,
catalog, channel and capture serve the desktop (portal), the bench and trajetorias (the bench's
automated replies in Veredas 2); the decision client serves the desktop against trajetorias'
service; the PCG32 serves the bench and the sort plan. Every module has two Veredas 2 consumers
or sits between two, so all of it moves (`docs/core-plan.md`, the table).

Moved as Veredas 1 had it: `SourceId`, `Value` (with `as_f64` and `Display`), `SeriesType`,
`SeriesRef`, `Sample` (with `index_ms`, `series_ref`), `now_unix_ms`, `SourceHandle` (new, id,
request_stop, is_finished, poll_outcome, join, the signalling drop), `stop_flag`, `SourceError`
(all six variants: the data plane's tail workers, portal's next, raise `Io`, `Pattern`, `Spec`,
`Backfill`).

Changed: `Sample` and `SeriesType` derive `PartialEq`/`Hash` for exact assertions; `Quality` is
new (Veredas 1 judged an OPC read from its `StatusCode` alone; the plan names quality a shared
type); `poll_outcome` takes the finished join with `Option::take_if` instead of an
`unwrap_or_else(unreachable!)`, no unwrap on the path.

Left with the data plane (portal, P-002): `LineEvent`, the `Source` trait, `Emitter`,
`SourceSet`, `WorkerReport`, `DERIVED_SOURCE`, the store trait and its `Point`/`SeriesInfo`.
Veredas 1 tested `SourceHandle` through `SourceSet`; `tests/source_handle.rs` drives the handle
directly, on real threads.

The foundation's own rule no law checks, a library and never an executable, is
`tests/foundation.rs`: the manifest declares no `[[bin]]`/`[[example]]` and the disk holds no
`src/main.rs`, `src/bin/` or `examples/` (the checks are themselves tested on a planted folder).
Veredas 1's `examples/demo_server.rs` is therefore not carried: the standing demo plant to drive
by hand is maquinar's.
