# C-145 evidence - the topology

## Nodes, links, rings, components (2026-11-25)

Phase 6's first piece. Absorbed from Veredas 1's `topology.rs` (`/home/thebdr/code/Veredas`,
branch `supervisao-plan`), changed on the way:
- **A name is never a key** (the plan's rule): Veredas 1 resolved a neighbour table's chassis by
  the station names devices gave by DCP as well as by the chassis they announced; here only by a
  MAC (the peer's port MAC that the port data gives, a device's own or the one device whose port
  it is) or the chassis id a device announces in its own LLDP, exactly one device announcing it.
- **A port that said it was leaving is no one's cable**, and does not make an interface a mirror;
  Veredas 1 drew it as long as it had been heard.
- **The MRP domain is the membership** (the plan's trap: MRRT's port status is not ring
  membership): Veredas 1 left out ports whose status word was 0, so a ring whose MRRT is off was
  missed.
- **A ring is closed only by one cycle through all its members**: Veredas 1's check (as many links
  as members, each of degree two) took two triangles for a ring of six.
- **Links inferred from a forwarding table** come with the SNMP phase, which brings the tables; the
  input is not carried until then.
- Mirror and hub interfaces, ambiguous chassis and a MAC against another device's chassis are
  said in notes, as Veredas 1 said the first two.

Before the first commit, 27 mutants of `topology.rs`: 23 killed by the first tests, four more once
tests were added (an all-zero MAC where a broken frame made a device of it, a triangle with a tail,
a device alone after a larger component, the links' order with chassis ends among them).

## The first refute round, answered (2026-11-25)

The critic (its own copy, an oracle written from the clause's words over 30 000 generated fusions
and neighbour sets, each in several orders, and 20 000 ring cases, 38 mutants) found the code
matching the oracle in every case, and refuted on what the test left unpinned and one gap in the
code:
- **The MAC taken over an ambiguous chassis was not said**: the note fired only when exactly one
  other device announced the chassis. It is said whenever another device announces it, and never
  where the MAC and the chassis agree.
- **A port heard alone on two interfaces got a cable on each**, each credited with all its frames
  (fusion keeps no count per interface). LLDP stops at the far end of one cable, so such a port is
  no one's cable: no link, a note (a hub or a mirror joins the interfaces).
- **A word no read said** (no witnesses) was drawn, and **one from a device nothing heard** drew a
  link to no node: neither is drawn, the second said.
- **Interfaces only DCP or ARP was heard on** were no nodes; Veredas 1 drew them and the clause
  says "heard": they are nodes now (without a cable).
- **Ten mutants the test let through** (the system name taken as a key, two ports of one device
  counted as one sender, only MRRT off a member, every domain a port ever stated, the port owner
  before a device's own MAC, notes said when not so, the neighbours unsorted with notes in play,
  chassis links left out of components, a port id cut at any dot, a self-link opening a ring) are
  each pinned by a test now.

Of 43 mutants of the answer (the first round's 27, the critic's survivors, and the new paths: the
port on two interfaces drawn or unsaid, a word no read said drawn, a report from a device nothing
heard drawn, the interfaces only DCP was heard on no nodes), all are killed.

## The second refute round, answered (2026-11-26)

The critic (its own copy; an oracle from the raw observations over 60 000 generated cases, a third
of them rings, each with the neighbours shuffled four times and fusion's order twice; 24 mutants)
found the code agreeing with its oracle and deterministic in every order, and refuted on what the
test left unpinned (19 of 24 mutants survived) and two gaps in the code's reading of the clause:
- **A port that left and came back on another interface** was read as a hub joining both, and took
  back the cable of the interface it had left (`heard_on` is a set over all time; leaving retired a
  port's cable but not its earlier hearings). Fusion now keeps, for each port, the frames heard on
  each interface and the place of the last (`on`, written in the scan file), and the topology
  counts only the interfaces heard since the port last said it was leaving: that port has moved,
  and the interface it left is the other port's cable. A heard link's witnesses are the frames
  heard on its interface (they were the port's frames on any).
- **A chassis id was matched only against chassis announced as a local id**, so a chassis two
  devices announce under two subtypes resolved to one of them, and one announced as an interface
  name by one device was not identified. A port data's peer chassis is a bare text (the record
  carries no subtype), so a chassis is its text whatever the subtype, a chassis node is
  `chassis:<text>`, and an empty chassis id is none.
- **Notes** named a leaving port's device by its label (two devices of one label said the same
  sentence) and a reporter nothing heard once per word: each names the device by its MAC and is
  said once.
- **An interface named twice** kept the last entry's MAC (none): it keeps the one it is given.
- What the clause did not rule on is ruled now and pinned: a leaving port stays its device's ring
  membership (the ring is its configuration, left open); a chassis a device announced before
  another is still its own.
- The survivors are each pinned (the second round's cases in the clause's Verification): a leaving
  port beside an active one, a port back after leaving, the three paths of the MAC-taken note,
  three words of one port alike but for their chassis in every order, the components' tie-break,
  the domain stated last after a change back, two pairs of double cables, a dotted station name
  in a port id, the reporting port's id cut, a word no read said, the heard link's frames.

Of 30 mutants of the answer (the critic's survivors written against the new code, and the new
paths: the interfaces since leaving taken inclusive, a port's per-interface frames or last place
replaced rather than added, the heard link's witnesses all the port's frames, notes repeated, an
empty chassis claimed or made a node, an interface named twice losing its MAC, a leaving note by
label, the chassis text taken with its subtype, a chassis link keeping the subtype), all are
killed; two (three words of one port alike but for their chassis, an unknown reporter's port id
given whole) only once a case was added for each. The critic's m51 (a hub note for a leaving port
too) has no place to go in the answer: a leaving port is heard on no interface since it left. Its
m52 (the confirmation counters swapped) is equivalent.
