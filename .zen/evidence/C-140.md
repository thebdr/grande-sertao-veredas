# C-140 evidence - what a frame says

## The observations (2026-11-24)

Phase 4's second piece, on the wire seam (C-139): what each kept frame says about one device, with
its witness. Absorbed from Veredas 1's `observe.rs` (`/home/thebdr/code/Veredas`, branch
`supervisao-plan`), changed on the way:
- **The witness's time is the frame's nanosecond stamp** and its frame index a `u64`, from the seam.
- **Damage is a list of named problems**, each kept (Veredas 1 kept one string); a DCP block that
  does not fit its kind is named with its option and suboption while the rest is observed (the
  codec keeps it damaged, C-132), and an LLDP PDU that is none (an unfit mandatory TLV in its place,
  C-133's sixth round) observes nothing.
- **An LLDP frame or an ARP sender from a group address** is damage too (Veredas 1 checked DCP's
  source alone); an ARP packet that is not Ethernet's and IPv4's, or of another operation, is
  damage named, where Veredas 1 passed over it silently.
- **The class is a type**, named in a count as Veredas 1's strings were (`dcp` for its
  `profinet-dcp`, `cyclic` for `profinet-rt`, with the alarms and the other PROFINET ids apart), read
  back from its name, an unknown EtherType as `other:0x....`.
- The subject's rule (the MAC a frame names for its device) is kept: an LLDP PDU's chassis MAC TLV,
  else a MAC chassis id, else its source; fusion (next) matches a local chassis id to a station
  name it already knows.

Before the first commit, 18 mutants of `observe.rs` (each rule of what is observed, each group
source refusal, each ARP check, the classes of each PROFINET range and two EtherTypes, the subject's
order, the damage of a block, the witness's index) were run against the tests: 17 killed, and the
subject's order (a MAC chassis id read before the chassis MAC TLV) killed once a PDU holding both,
differing, was added.

## Refute round 1 (2026-11-24): refuted, a request read as an answer, a cut frame read whole

The critic swept every EtherType (untagged, one and two tags), every PROFINET frame id against
tshark (the ranges agree exactly), DCP service and service type bytes, ARP fields, every group bit
of every source, every LLDP chassis subtype, every cut, and 400 000 mutated frames: no panic, the
names read back, the counts right. Found, and answered:
- **A request read as an answer** (the defect): a request was any PDU of service type 0 only, so a
  service type of 2, 4 or 6 (which the codec reads as a request by its selection bit) was heard as
  an answer, its filter taken for the asker's identity: a scanner's MAC named as the station it
  looked for, a false duplicate name in fusion. And a service DCP does not define was observed. A
  request is now known by its selection bit, and a service other than 3 to 6 or a service type
  other than 0, 1 and 5 (which tshark leaves undecoded) says nothing, named. Every service and
  service type byte is swept on two shapes.
- **A frame the capture cut read whole** (the defect): an LLDP frame cut at a TLV boundary decoded
  as a whole PDU, its chassis MAC TLV left out, so the observation named the port's MAC for its
  device. A cut frame (`Frame::is_cut`, C-139) now says nothing, named with the bytes kept, and is
  classed still.
- **A group address as a chassis**: an LLDP PDU's chassis MAC TLV or MAC chassis id of a group
  address made a subject no device is; now named, nothing observed. The noise test's exemption for
  LLDP subjects is gone.
- **Four ARP rules unpinned**: an unpadded 42-byte frame, an operation of 0, a protocol of 0x86DD and
  a hardware address length of 8 are now held.
- **Class names that are none read back**: `other:0x+123`, upper-case digits, and a type with a class
  of its own (`other:0x0800`) are refused; a name is the class's own exactly.
- **Bytes after a DCP PDU's last block** went unseen: named, the PDU still observed.
- Wording: "under two tags" was two single tags; a frame under a second tag is classed by its type.
Before this commit, twelve mutants of the new rules were run: all killed.
