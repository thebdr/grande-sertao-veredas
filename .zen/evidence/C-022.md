# C-022: The poll worker: lab notebook

## The port (2026-11-06)

From Veredas 1's `crates/veredas-opc/src/worker.rs` with its inline tests and
`tests/worker_e2e.rs`. They are now `src/opc/worker.rs` and one file, `tests/opc_worker.rs`.

What changed (the first four are hazards in Veredas 1, worth porting back by whoever owns it):
- **No request waits for a connection.** A write asked of a worker with no session (connecting,
  or waiting out its backoff) is answered at once with `NotConnected`, and so is a read.
  - Veredas 1 left both in the queue until the next session existed.
  - So a command pulse asked for during an outage fired whenever the plant returned, minutes
    later.
  - Its caller meanwhile waited with no bound.
- **The worker checks the master switch again just before the wire.** Veredas 1 checked it only
  in the handle, so a write already queued behind a poll still fired after the switch was turned
  off.
- **Every connection attempt is bounded (10 s) and raced against stop.** Veredas 1 awaited the
  connect unbounded and unraced. A peer that accepts TCP and never answers held the worker, and
  a stop with it.
- **A zero poll interval is refused at spawn.** Veredas 1 panicked the worker thread in tokio's
  `interval`.
- **A session abandoned half-way has its event loop aborted.** Veredas 1 dropped the join handle,
  which detaches the task.
- **Errors are typed.** `OpcError` covers the worker's text, and `WriteRefused::Transport` carries
  one. Writes are answered with `WriteRefused`.
- **`TagRead::quality` added.** Good only with a value and a good status; uncertain when the
  server says so; bad otherwise.
- **Jobs and the stop are raced.** Veredas 1 woke a 200 ms sleep to notice the stop.
- **The read constants are public:** `MAX_NODES_PER_READ`, `CALL_TIMEOUT`, `CONNECT_TIMEOUT` and
  the retry bounds.

The chunked read is now proven, not assumed. The demo plant caps a Read at 1 000 nodes and polls
1 200, so an unchunked poll is refused whole (C-023). In Veredas 1 the demo server took any size,
and the chunk test passed with or without chunking.

New tests:
- a queued write disarmed while it waits behind the slow tag, and its armed control;
- the outage write, never landing after the plant returns;
- the silent server, and the stop mid-connect;
- the exact backoff;
- user and password through the worker;
- both clocks;
- a dropped consumer;
- quality;
- per-write statuses in order;
- the zero interval.
