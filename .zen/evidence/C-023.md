# C-023: What the OPC UA stack does: lab notebook

## The port (2026-11-06)

From Veredas 1's `crates/veredas-opc/tests/capabilities.rs`, now `tests/opc_capabilities.rs`,
against `opc::demo` (Veredas 1's `demo.rs`).

The demo plant changed:
- **A per-call Read cap of 1 000 nodes,** under its 1 200-node bulk folder.
- **A slow tag,** whose read callback blocks for 400 ms.
- **The namespace index is held.** Veredas 1 looked it up again and `unreachable!`'d.
- **Startup errors are typed** (`DemoError`).
- **Comments name no vendor or product.**

New pins:
- **A Read past the cap is refused whole.** The stack reports the server's `BadTooManyOperations`
  fault as `BadDecodingError: Request returned ServiceFault`. That is the text a field server's
  refusal showed in Veredas 1's pilot, so the stack, not the server, loses the status.
- **One read at the cap succeeds, and the session survives** the refusal.
- **A Browse pages at what was asked** (500, then two BrowseNext rounds), and a point can be
  released.
- **Status severity is the top two bits,** the reserved 0b11 reading as bad, and a status
  displays as its name. `Quality` and the failure texts rest on this.
- **The slow tag takes its time.**
- **An unknown user is refused,** like a wrong password.
