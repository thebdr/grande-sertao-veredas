## Refute round 1 (2026-11-06): refuted; the port note above was wrong about the stack

- **The read-cap pin tested the wrong limit.** The stack refuses any array over
  `MAX_ARRAY_LENGTH` = 1 000 at the server's decoder, before the per-call cap is checked. The
  demo cap was also 1 000, so deleting the cap left the pin green.
  - The stack does NOT lose the server's status. A Read past a real cap fails as
    `BadTooManyOperations: Request returned ServiceFault`.
  - `BadDecodingError` is the decoder limit: what the field's 3,048-tag source met.
  - The demo cap is now 800, under the limit, and pinned with its own status.
  - The decoder limit is pinned apart (1 001 nodes), with the chunk asserted under it.
- **Test gaps, each pinned now:**

  - a released point actually gone (Good, then `BadContinuationPointInvalid`);
  - the unknown user refused within 10 s;
