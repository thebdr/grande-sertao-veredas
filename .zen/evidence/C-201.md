# C-201 evidence - the test file and the function file, read and checked

## The design (2026-11-22)

`docs/test-schema.md` is the reference; Q310 (the syntax), Q311 (the script box's language) and
Q317 (how plant data is named) are taken as the assistant recommends, so the reading is one
front end over a model the engine runs (the orchestrator's word: a ruling for another syntax
rewrites `lex` and `parse` and the examples, not the engine).

- `lex` never fails: what it cannot read is a `Bad` token with its reason, reported where the
  parser meets it. Line ends are tokens; runs of them are one, and the line end before a line
  that opens with `->` is dropped, so a call's outputs may sit on the line after it (the
  design's own layout).
- `parse` cuts the header at the first line opening with the word `test` or `function`, hands it
  to `veredas_core::schema::check` (the suite's texts) and refuses any other key in it; then
  reads the body by recursive descent. Inside parentheses the parser skips line ends itself, so
  a setting that takes the rest of its line (`id`, `area`, `portal`, `use`) is cut from the text
  by its span and cannot leave the lexer counting an open parenthesis.
- `check` resolves every `<name>.<column>`: a variable's row column when the name is a variable
  of the scope (the test's: constants, a script's assignments and loops, `read`, `await` and
  call outputs; a function's: ports, assignments, loops, awaits, call outputs), plant data
  otherwise, rewritten into `ExprKind::Plant` in the model it returns, so the engine never
  guesses. Two functions of one name: the first wins and the second is the problem.

## What the build met

- **`max` was a keyword and a built-in.** `retry a max 1` read it as a word of the language, and
  `max(a, b)` then could not be called. It is no longer reserved: the route reads it by its
  place. The reference's list of words lost it; the test holds the list equal to the reader's.
- **A step named `report`.** The design's mock names a step after its action; the reference's
  worked example did the same and the reader refused it as a word of the language. The step is
  `summary` in the reference: names stay apart from the language's words.
- **A setting after `stop`.** A line `area late` after a step ending in `stop` was reported as
  "nothing follows `stop`" before the setting rule; a setting's key now answers first.
- **The function a duplicate name resolves to.** The table of functions kept the last of two of
  one name, while the problem pointed back at the first; it keeps the first.
